# Conventions

## Language
- **Content**: All user-facing content and technical descriptions (like `llms.txt`) are in **Portuguese (PT-BR)**.
- **Filenames**: Mix of Portuguese descriptive names and standard web technical names (e.g., `index.html`).

## SEO Standards
- **Meta Hierarchy**: Explicitly planned via spreadsheets for H1, H2, and Title tags.
- **Categorization**: Services are clearly categorized (Eletricista, Encanador, Marido de Aluguel).

## Coding Style
- **HTML**: Standard HTML5 used in `linktree` and `avaliar` stubs.
- **Naming**: Use of hyphens and underscores in image names for SEO optimization.
